A MINUTE TO LEARN. A WHILE TO MASTER.

Let’s connect the dots.
And the squares.

01

Find something in common.

A line shares one color or one shape. Each tile in that line must be different. Six tiles is the maximum.

02

Make your move.

Select a rack tile, then a highlighted space. Play one or more tiles in one connected row or column. Every touching line must be valid. Click a staged tile to take it back.

03

Make it count.

Score each tile in every line you create or extend. A full line of six earns 12 points. Use all your tiles after the bag empties for a final 6-point bonus.

A few table rules

The largest matching hand starts; ties go to you. Start with that full set. Swap tiles instead of playing to refresh your hand. When the bag is empty, pass only if you have no move; two passes end the game. Highest score wins.
